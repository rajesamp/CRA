# Postmortem INC-2201: checkout-service payment timeout outage

Service: checkout-service. Change type: Config change. Severity: SEV1. Date: 2026-02-14.

## Summary

A configuration change to checkout-service lowered the timeout on its calls to
payment-gateway from 5000 ms to 500 ms. Staging traffic did not expose the
problem. During a promotional traffic spike, the short timeout turned slow
payment responses into failures. Checkout success dropped by 42% for about 38
minutes until the change was rolled back. About 1,150 checkout attempts failed
or were significantly delayed. No data was lost and no payment information was
exposed.

## Timeline (UTC)

14:02 the config change deployed through the standard release process. 14:10 a
promotional email drove a 3x traffic increase. 14:15 the payment failure alert
fired. 14:31 the reduced payment-gateway timeout was identified as the cause.
14:40 the change was rolled back; failure rates returned to baseline within six
minutes.

## Root cause

The timeout change was classified as a low-risk config tweak and was not load
tested at production traffic levels. The change review did not flag
checkout-service's dependency on payment-gateway as high-risk, even though two
earlier incidents involved the same dependency: INC-1987 (config change, SEV2,
2025-09-02) and INC-2055 (dependency upgrade, SEV1, 2025-11-19).

## Action items

Load test any config change touching payment-gateway timeouts at 3x baseline
traffic. Flag checkout-service and payment-gateway as high-risk services in
change review. Add an automated rollback trigger when checkout success stays
below 90% for more than five minutes.

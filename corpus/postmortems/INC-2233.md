# Postmortem INC-2233: checkout-service flag enabled for 100% before canary completed

Service: checkout-service. Change type: Feature flag rollout. Severity: SEV2. Date: 2026-04-22.

## Summary

A new checkout flow behind a feature flag was switched to 100% of traffic
while its canary was still running. A bug in the new flow's address
validation rejected valid orders from some regions for about 40 minutes.

## Root cause

Flag enabled for 100% before the canary completed. The rollout skipped the
staged percentages because the canary showed no errors in its first minutes.

## Action items

Require the canary to complete before widening a flag. Roll out checkout flags
in stages (1%, 10%, 50%, 100%) with a hold at each step.

# Postmortem INC-2318: payment-gateway rate limit lowered below peak traffic

Service: payment-gateway. Change type: Config change. Severity: SEV1. Date: 2026-08-15.

## Summary

payment-gateway's inbound rate limit was lowered to protect the card processor
during a vendor maintenance window. The new limit sat below normal peak
traffic, and when evening traffic arrived, legitimate payment requests were
rejected with rate-limit errors for about 30 minutes.

## Root cause

Rate limit lowered below peak traffic needs. The limit was sized from a
weekday average rather than the peak, and the change was not checked against
traffic data.

## Action items

Size rate limits against peak traffic with headroom. Treat payment-gateway
config changes as high-risk, consistent with the INC-2201 action items.
